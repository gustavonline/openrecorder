# Support

OpenRecorder is a free preview maintained by one person. Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/gustavonline/openrecorder/issues) for bugs and questions, after reading [docs/install.md](docs/install.md) and [docs/update-uninstall-recovery.md](docs/update-uninstall-recovery.md).

A useful report has: your OS and chip, the OpenRecorder version (Settings → About), what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the output of `OpenRecorder --doctor`. Do not attach recordings, access codes, links or the contents of `native-events.jsonl` without reading them first.

What is not supported: macOS earlier than 26.0 (the bundled media helper requires it), Windows and Linux builds (experimental, never run on hardware), recording while another app owns the camera or microphone, public sharing on networks that block Cloudflare tunnels, and notarization (the macOS app is not notarized).
